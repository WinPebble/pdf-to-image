# Privacy

WinPebble PDF to Image processes PDF files locally on the user's Windows device.

- No telemetry is collected.
- No user account is required.
- PDF contents and generated images are not uploaded or transmitted by the application.
- Conversion does not require Internet access.
- The application does not run a background service.

**Network policy:** This program will not transfer any information to other networked systems unless specifically requested by the user or the person installing or operating it.

The WinPebble website has its own privacy information at https://winpebble.com/privacy/.
